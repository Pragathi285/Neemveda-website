Where we ship

We currently ship to most serviceable PIN codes across India. If your PIN code is not serviceable, we'll let you know and refund any payment in full.

Dispatch and delivery times

  • Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days of being placed.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders of ₹499 and above. A flat delivery fee of ₹50 applies to smaller orders. Any charges are shown clearly at checkout before you pay.

Tracking your order

Once your order ships, you'll receive the tracking details by email. You can also follow your order in My account → Orders or on the Track order page.
