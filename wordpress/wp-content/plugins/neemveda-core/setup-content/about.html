Why we began

A ritual worth remembering

Many of us remember a grandparent standing in the courtyard with a neem twig, unhurried, starting the day the way their own parents had. It was simple, local and kind to the earth.

Today, most of us brush with plastic that will outlive us by centuries. neemveda began with a small question: what if the old way still makes sense?

From tree to toothbrush

Carefully selected, simply prepared

  • Sourced with care. Twigs are cut from mature neem trees without harming the tree.
  • Cleaned and sorted by hand for size and straightness.
  • Gently dried so they keep well on your shelf.
  • Packed plastic-free, tied with jute in a recyclable paper box.
