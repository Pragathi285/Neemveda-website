What we collect

  • When you order: your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address and the items you buy. We take Cash on Delivery; we don't collect card, UPI or bank details.
  • Delivery pin: if you drop a pin on the map or tap "Use my current location" at checkout, we save that map position with your order so the courier can find you. It's optional: you can order with your address only.
  • When you create a profile: your name, mobile number, email address and a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form). We send a one-time code to confirm your email (and your mobile number, when SMS verification is on).
  • When you contact us or join the newsletter: what you send us, and your email address.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to stop abuse (for example, too many code requests or login attempts).

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack, deliver and support your orders, and to send order and delivery updates.
  • To verify your email or mobile number and keep your account secure.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you subscribed.

Who we share it with

Only what each partner needs to do its job. We never sell your personal information.

  • Courier partners: name, phone, address and delivery pin, to deliver your order.
  • Email delivery provider (Brevo): your email address and the message, to send order updates and codes.
  • SMS provider (when phone verification is on): your mobile number, to send a one-time code.
  • Push notifications (OneSignal): only if you choose "Allow" for order updates in your browser.
  • Maps and fonts: the checkout map is loaded from OpenStreetMap and unpkg, and the website fonts from Google Fonts. These services see your IP address when your browser loads them.

Cookies and storage

We use only cookies needed for the shop to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your cart, and remembering your choice on the "Create your profile / Continue as guest" box. Your browser also stores small settings, such as whether you closed the notification prompt. We don't use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require. Profiles are kept until you ask us to delete them. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. One-time codes expire after 10 minutes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent (for example, to stop the newsletter), by writing to support@neemveda.in. You can update your details yourself in My account. To unsubscribe from the newsletter, reply "unsubscribe" to any newsletter email or write to us.
