How to use

Four simple steps to a fresher smile

Using a neem stick takes a minute to learn and becomes a calm morning ritual.

  1. 1

    Rinse the stick

    Wash the neem stick under clean running water.

  2. 2

    Chew one end

    Gently chew one end until soft, brush-like bristles form.

  3. 3

    Brush gently

    Brush your teeth and along the gums in soft, circular strokes. Rinse your mouth well.

  4. 4

    Trim and reuse

    After a few days, trim off the used end and chew a fresh one.

Tip: Rinse the stick after each use and let it air-dry. When it gets too short to hold comfortably, it can go straight into your compost or garden soil.

Our heritage

The story of the datun

Not so long ago, mornings across India began under a neem tree. A fresh twig, snapped and chewed into a soft brush, was the datun: a ritual shared by grandparents, farmers and schoolchildren alike.

Ayurvedic texts describe dantadhavana, the cleaning of teeth with twigs of trees such as neem. Somewhere along the way, plastic took its place.

neemveda brings the datun home again: carefully selected, cleaned and packed, ready for modern mornings.

Frequently asked questions

How should I store my neem sticks?

Keep them in their box in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture. After each use, rinse the stick and let it air-dry standing upright. Avoid closed, damp containers.

How long does one neem stick last?

One stick can usually be used for a few days. Trim off the used, bristled end and chew a fresh one as you go, until the stick becomes too short to hold comfortably. [PLACEHOLDER] Confirm the typical number of days for your sticks.

Are neem sticks suitable for children?

Older children can use neem sticks under adult supervision, once they can chew and brush gently on their own. [PLACEHOLDER] Add your recommended minimum age. Neem has a naturally bitter taste, which some children take a little time to enjoy. If in doubt, please check with your family dentist.

Do I need toothpaste with a neem stick?

Traditionally, neem sticks are used on their own. You are welcome to continue with your usual toothpaste or tooth powder if you prefer. And please keep up your regular dental check-ups.
